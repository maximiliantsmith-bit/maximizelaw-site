How we start

"I'd rather not hand you a method before we know what the problem is."

  1. A free conversation

    We talk about your goals, your timeline and what has or hasn't worked so far.

  2. A diagnosis

    We look at your work together and find where the points are going, before choosing any method.

  3. A plan you can keep

    Steady weekly practice, untimed first and timed later, with a review of every miss.

About Maximilian

Maximilian Smith is a licensed Illinois attorney and independent tutor. He spent more than a decade at The Princeton Review, first teaching the LSAT and other admissions tests, then leading national operations for its graduate programs.

He designed more than 40 instructor trainings and onboarded more than 500 instructors. Before that, he ran a small law practice. He lives in Forest Park, Illinois, and works with students online and in person.
